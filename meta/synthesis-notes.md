### Versioning Pattern
Many projects have v1, v2, v3... across accounts. The latest version number is NOT always the best — earlier versions sometimes have cleaner architecture or working features that got broken in rewrites.

### Account Migration Pattern
Work often starts in GenJess, gets refined in agenticjess-star, then production copies land in jGPT-Automated. Worth tracking.
