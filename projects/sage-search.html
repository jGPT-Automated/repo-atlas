The Thesis

Everyone has thousands of bookmarks, screenshots, saved links, and random content scattered across devices. Finding anything is impossible, and manual organization is dead — nobody actually files things into folders. Sage Search's bet is that people don't want better organization; they want organization removed as a task entirely. Share it the way you already share things — a screenshot, a link, a forward — and let the system do the rest.

02

Architecture

Ingestion
Telegram Bot API
Reuses the share-sheet gesture people already do — no new app or workflow to learn.
Content Processing
Gemini · pdf.js-extract · Mammoth
Text, images (vision-described), URLs (scraped), PDFs and DOCX (text-extracted), video (frame-described).
Embeddings
Gemini Embeddings
Every processed item converted to a vector for semantic — not keyword — search.
Storage
Firestore · Cloud Storage
Structured metadata in Firestore, media files in Cloud Storage, per-user collections.
Search
Cosine Similarity
Natural-language queries ranked against stored embeddings — "that article about AI" just works.
Web Scraping
Cheerio · Axios
A shared URL is fetched, parsed, and its meaningful content extracted before embedding.
03

Core Loop

Save
Share anything to @sagesearch_bot. No categorization, no tagging, no decisions at capture time.
Forget
The whole point — you don't have to remember where you put it, or that you filed it at all.
Query
"Show me that funny meme from last week" or "what did I save about startups" — plain language, not search operators.
Delight
The moment the right thing surfaces instantly, from a pile you never organized — that's the product.
04

Market Position

Validation
Scored 8/10 in market research — the competitive field (Notion, Obsidian, Rewind, Mem) is fragmented, and every incumbent trades off usability, privacy, or performance to get their features.
Why Telegram First
Fast to build (simple bot API, no app store approval), fast to validate with real users and real usage patterns, and fast to iterate directly on feedback.
MVP Scope
Receive messages, process text/images/URLs, store with embeddings, search via bot commands, return relevant results — deliberately nothing more.
Next Milestone
3-5 early adopters testing the core save/search cycle, then expanding to 10-15 users for a week of real daily usage.
