The Thesis

Most small trades businesses are stuck between two bad options: freelancers who execute without strategy, and boutique agencies priced out of reach. Outreach OS's bet is to do the work first — build a genuinely better site on spec, verify it actually beats the business's current site and their competitors — and only then reach out, with real, finished proof instead of a pitch deck. If they say no, they still walk away with something real that was already built for free.

02

The Pipeline

1. Prospector
Finds businesses with a weak or nonexistent web presence — the actual targeting layer.
2. Strategist
Audits the business's current site and its competitors to define what "better" actually means here.
3. Builder
Rapid-prototypes a real site from templates and design systems — never free-codes — and deploys it live to a Vercel subdomain.
4. Auditor
Verifies the finished site actually beats the competition — the claim has to be true before outreach ever happens.
5. Packager
Generates a video walkthrough of the new site and drafts the outreach message.
6. Persona Tester
Reads the outreach email as the business owner would before it ever sends — catches anything that would kill trust.
7. Conductor
Runs the whole pipeline end to end, coordinating all six other agents.

Proof of Concept

BNR Associates
The pipeline's first real output — a power-washing business site — is independently rated the best-designed site in its category, validating the core claim that the pipeline can actually out-build what's already out there.
Pricing Position
$1,997–2,497 for the initial build — deliberately in the gap between freelancers (no strategy) and boutique agencies (out of reach for small trades businesses).
The Pitch That Doesn't Feel Like a Pitch
Because the work is already done and live before the first message is sent, the ask isn't "trust us with a project" — it's "here's what we built, tell us if you want it."
Next Milestone
