Architecture

AI Consultant
GPT-4.1 · Voice-First
"OV" — the AI consultant. Guides onboarding via voice, recommends supplement routines, guides checkout. Optimizes for re-subscription while genuinely helping.
Voice Layer
ElevenLabs WebRTC
Real-time voice interaction. Users talk to OV naturally — no forms, no friction. WebRTC for low-latency conversation.
Backend
FastAPI · Supabase
Postgres for user profiles, supplement tracking, biometric data. FastAPI handles AI orchestration and supplement matching.
Dashboard
React · Real-time Trends
Post-purchase dashboard: track supplements, biometrics (Apple Watch integration), and trend visualization over time.
Community
Privacy-First Matching
Color-gradient matching of supplement stacks. Users see who has similar routines without revealing personal health data.
Monetization
Freemium → Premium AI
Free tier: basic tracking. Premium: AI consultant, trend analysis, personalized optimization. Affiliate revenue from supplement partners.
02

Product Vision

Voice-First Onboarding
Users describe their wellness goals via voice. OV interprets intent, asks clarifying questions, and recommends a personalized supplement routine — no questionnaires.
6 Precision Formulas
Curated supplement formulations targeting specific wellness vectors. Each formula has a documented mechanism, expected outcomes, and tracking metrics.
Biometric Tracking + Apple Watch
Post-purchase dashboard tracks supplement adherence and biometric trends. Apple Watch integration for heart rate, sleep, and activity data correlation.
Privacy-First Community
Color-gradient matching shows users who share similar supplement stacks. No personal health data exposed — only the gradient pattern. Creates social accountability without privacy violation.
Stealth Retention Engine
Agent quietly optimizes for re-subscription and upsell while providing genuine value. Balances user benefit with business sustainability — the retention mechanic that traditional dropshipping lacks.
