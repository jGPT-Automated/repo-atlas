# ProPersona — Interactive Resume with Voice

**Repo:** GenJess/pro-persona

**Score:** 4.4/5  
**Category:** Portfolio / Hiring

## What It Is
Transform a static resume into an **interactive, voice-responsive AI persona** powered by ElevenLabs conversational AI.

**Problem It Solves:**
- Resumes are passive documents
- Recruiters skim; hiring managers scan
- Your professional story gets lost in PDFs
- No way to make yourself memorable as a candidate

**Solution:**
- Upload your resume
- AI extracts your background, skills, experience
- ElevenLabs conversational AI brings it to life
- Interviewers can ask questions → AI answers from your resume

## The Tech
- **Frontend:** React + Vite + shadcn/ui (TypeScript)
- **Voice:** ElevenLabs conversational API
- **Deployment:** Lovable.app (live, serverless)
