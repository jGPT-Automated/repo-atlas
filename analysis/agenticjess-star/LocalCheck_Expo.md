## Overview
LocalCheck is a **street sports social app** for discovering courts, checking in, joining game runs, and competing in ELO-ranked leaderboards. The app targets athletes who want to find pickup games in real-time.

### Core Features
1. **Map view** — Full-screen court discovery with live player counts
2. **Court profiles** — Court details, schedule, upcoming runs, check-in roster
3. **Game runs** — Matchmaking lobby with team balancing and RSVP
4. **ELO leaderboard** — Competitive ranking system (BRONZE→SILVER→GOLD→PLATINUM tiers)
5. **Player profiles** — Head-to-head stats, match history, friend connections
6. **Check-in flow** — One-tap status broadcast to court and feed
7. **Log game results** — Record wins/losses to update ELO immediately

### Design Language
**Dark editorial brutalism** — inspired by Nike SNKRS and Victory Journal
- Dark backgrounds (#0D0D10) with high-contrast white text
- Orange accent (#FF5500) for live states and actions
- Oswald typography (all-caps headings) + Inter body text
- Minimal decoration; information-dense UI with 1px borders
