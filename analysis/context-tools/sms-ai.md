## What It Is
TextBack — human-paced AI texting service. SMS personas (coach, friend, therapist, adversary) with deliberately delayed responses (4–20 hours randomized). No app required, works in native Messages.

## Business Model
- $15/mo (1 persona) / $25/mo (full roster of 5)
- Subscription, no app friction, native SMS
- Persistent cross-thread memory
